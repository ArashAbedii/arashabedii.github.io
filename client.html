Questions

Good to know

Who owns the website and the accounts?

You do. Your domain, website, Google Business profile and social accounts are in your name, and you have full access at all times.

Do I have to sign up for a monthly plan?

No. You can just have your website built. Monthly plans are optional and you can cancel any time with one month's notice.

How long does a website take?

A business website usually takes 3–4 weeks. A website with bookings and payments usually takes 5–8 weeks, depending on what you need.

How do payments work?

For a build: 50% to start and 50% at launch. Monthly plans are paid at the start of each month. We'll agree a payment method that works for you.
